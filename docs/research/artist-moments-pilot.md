# Artist moments pilot

Research date: 2026-09-06. Editorial research draft for review, not an implementation specification. No app content has been published. Article-supported facts below have not yet been matched to database record IDs or independently checked against broadcaster archives.

Pilot: CORTIS, aespa, BTS, IVE, Red Velvet, T-ara.

## CORTIS: their first trophy

Event: 2026-04-30, M Countdown, REDRED.

Draft blurb: CORTIS earned their first music show trophy with "REDRED" on April 30, 2026. The group won M Countdown with 8,902 points, and the broadcast included their first winning encore.

Source: [Soompi, first win report](https://www.soompi.com/article/1837470wpp/watch-cortis-takes-1st-ever-win-on-m-countdown-with-redred-performances-by-illit-katseye-and-more), E Cha, published 2026-05-01. The text explicitly identifies April 30 as the episode date and links the performance, win and encore.

Assessment: clear milestone. Source does not establish personal meaning beyond the first trophy. Event and publication dates differ.

## aespa: Black Mamba earns their first win

Event: 2021-01-17, Inkigayo, Black Mamba.

Draft blurb: "Black Mamba" brought aespa their first music show win on January 17, 2021. The group took first place on Inkigayo, ahead of KyoungSeo's "Shiny Star" and Rain and Park Jin Young's "Switch to Me."

Source: [Soompi, first win report](https://www.soompi.com/article/1449110wpp/aespa-takes-1st-ever-win-with-black-mamba-on-inkigayo), U Kim, published 2021-01-17. Headline identifies the first-ever win; body confirms the song, show and other placings.

Additional evidence: [Kstyle's contemporary report](https://kstyle.com/article.ksn?articleNo=2160415), published 2021-01-17, explicitly identifies a win without an appearance in its headline. [MataMata's report](https://www.matamata.com/kpop/2021/01/18/083816/2-bulan-debut-aespa-raih-kemenangan-pertama-dengan-lagu-black-mamba), published 2021-01-18, says Minhyuk, Naeun and Jaehyun filled the encore in aespa's absence and attributes that absence to promotions having ended. The broadcast itself has not yet been visually reviewed.

Revised draft blurb: aespa earned their first music show trophy with "Black Mamba" on Inkigayo on January 17, 2021, without appearing on the show. Contemporary coverage reports that hosts Minhyuk, Naeun and Jaehyun filled in for the encore.

User-provided research lead: Search Console shows interest in aespa's first win. The user remembers the hosts dancing to the song in the group's absence. This specific choreography detail still needs direct video verification. A [candidate recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w_bqDXwmhVY) has been located; it is an unofficial upload and has not been watched in this research pass. No evidence here establishes COVID restrictions as the reason for the group's absence, or the hosts' dance as the reason people search.

Assessment: prioritize a clearly labelled "aespa's first music show win" entry with the date, song, short context and a verified winner-announcement video. Small memorable broadcast details qualify alongside major career milestones. The existing generic first-win blurb above is superseded by this revised draft.

## BTS: what their first wins meant

Event: 2015-05-05, The Show, I Need U. Supporting interview discusses the opening run of wins through May 8.

Draft blurb: BTS earned their first music show trophy with "I Need U" on May 5, 2015. Reflecting on their first week of wins, Jimin said the recognition made their practice feel worthwhile, while Suga described a new responsibility to keep improving.

Sources:

- [Soompi, first-win report](https://www.soompi.com/article/728319wpp/bts-takes-first-ever-music-show-win-on-the-show-with%20-j%27ai%20besoin%20de%20toi), published 2015-05-05. Confirms first career win. Uses "I Need You" in its title; the app's song identity still needs matching.
- [Soompi, members reflect on winning](https://www.soompi.com/article/730053wpp/bts-sincerely-open-up-about-the-meaning-of-winning-1st-on-a-music-show), published 2015-05-09, reporting a May 8 MBN Star interview. Supports the paraphrases and distinguishes The Show on May 5, M Countdown on May 7 and Music Bank on May 8.

Assessment: strong story with attributed personal meaning. Interview remarks concern the opening run of wins, not a verified speech on the May 5 stage.

## IVE: a first win one week after debut

Event: 2021-12-08, Show Champion, ELEVEN.

Draft blurb: Just one week after debut, IVE won their first music show trophy with "ELEVEN" on Show Champion. Their first victory became an emotional moment for the group, captured in the broadcast's winner announcement and encore.

Source: [Soompi, first win report](https://www.soompi.com/article/1502797wpp/watch-ive-takes-tearful-1st-ever-win-on-show-champion-with-eleven-performances-by-everglow-oneus-and-more), E Cha, published 2021-12-08. Confirms the one-week interval and describes the emotional win.

Assessment: strong, concise timing milestone. No current or all-time "fastest group" claim is needed.

## Red Velvet: Ice Cream Cake wins their first trophy

Event: 2015-03-27, Music Bank, Ice Cream Cake.

Draft blurb: Red Velvet won their first music show trophy with "Ice Cream Cake" on Music Bank, a day after finishing second on M Countdown. The members celebrated with a tearful acceptance speech and encore.

Sources:

- [Soompi, first win report](https://www.soompi.com/article/714439wpp/red-velvet-gets-first-ever-music-show-win-on-music-bank-with-ice-cream-cake?mobile-app=true&theme=dark%2F1000), published 2015-03-27. Supports the first win, previous night's placing and tearful speech.
- [TenAsia, broadcast report](https://www.tenasia.co.kr/article/2015032783824), published 2015-03-27. Independently describes the victory and members' tears.

Assessment: a first-win story with more context than a date alone.

## T-ara: a win after more than five years

Event: 2017-06-20, The Show, What's My Name.

Draft blurb: "What's My Name" brought T-ara their first Korean music show win in over five years. Hyomin, Qri and Jiyeon cried on stage and struggled to give an acceptance speech. Eunjung later recalled crying when she heard the news by phone while filming a drama.

Sources:

- [Soompi, win report](https://www.soompi.com/article/1000581wpp/watch-t-ara-breaks-down-1st-win-whats-name-show-performances-ftisland-9muses-more), J K, published 2017-06-20. Identifies the previous win as "Lovey Dovey" in February 2012 and describes the three members' reaction.
- [Soompi, Eunjung interview report](https://www.soompi.com/article/1077723wpp/t-aras-eunjung-talks-love-members-emotional-1st-place-win), S Park, published 2017-11-15, reporting a November 14 interview. Supports Eunjung's absence, phone call and reaction.

Assessment: strongest example of why a single win matters. The song is "What's My Name," not "What's Your Name." The two articles support one combined story rather than two separate moments.

## Editorial recommendation

Start with the six main stories above, one per artist. BTS and T-ara demonstrate the value of pairing a result report with a later interview. The additional candidates provide expansion options without forcing a fixed quota.

Before an implementation handoff, remaining planning work is to match these events to existing app records and settle the presentation of a story with multiple sources. Broad article ingestion and statistics are outside this pilot. Source availability and source-supported claims are established here; publication readiness and database matching are not.
